# Schedule artwork prompts

## Header poster

Use the five official character references in this order: 嘉然、贝拉、乃琳、心宜、思诺. The 乃琳 source must be the cropped official front view (`乃琳.png`), never a fan-art image or the removed side view.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: very wide official-style website header illustration for an A-SOUL weekly schedule

Input images: Images 1–5 are the definitive official identity, facial-design, proportion, hair, accessory, costume, and color references for 嘉然, 贝拉, 乃琳, 心宜, and 思诺. Image 3 is the cropped official front view and the only allowed reference for 乃琳.

Primary request: Create one cohesive, polished official promotional-anime group key visual with exactly these five women, ordered left to right as 嘉然, 贝拉, 乃琳, 心宜, 思诺. Use the same mature, non-chibi rendering treatment for every character and do not introduce fan-art reinterpretations.

Height relationship: 乃琳, 贝拉, and 思诺 are approximately the same height. 心宜 is just slightly shorter. 嘉然 is just slightly shorter than 心宜. Keep every difference subtle (only a few percent), expressed through small eye-line and shoulder offsets rather than exaggerated tiers.

Character constraints:
- 嘉然: warm light-brown/orange long hair, curled ahoge, blue eyes, translucent lilac bow, faithful white/black/lilac outfit.
- 贝拉: long deep-violet hair, violet eyes, translucent violet upright ribbon ornaments, faithful white/black/violet outfit.
- 乃琳: mature adult facial proportions from the cropped official front view, long pearl-white hair, blue-violet eyes, silver star jewelry, faithful white/lilac/black outfit, normal human ears naturally hidden by hair.
- 心宜: short pink bob, pink eyes, faithful black/white/pink outfit.
- 思诺: long dark navy-violet hair, glasses, black beret with violet feather, white ruffled blouse, faithful black/violet outfit.

Scene/backdrop: bright warm ivory backdrop with sparse fine diagonal pink and violet lines and small restrained star accents.
Style/medium: cohesive official promotional anime key visual, mature character proportions, crisp continuous line art, restrained cel shading, stable local colors, consistent facial rendering and skin tone across all five, elegant but not glossy.
Composition/framing: extremely wide horizontal banner, target ratio about 3:1. Reserve the left 28-30% as quiet empty space for title text. Arrange all five characters as a close waist-up group on the right. Keep every face and signature accessory visible. Do not add text.
Lighting/mood: soft neutral daylight, bright and friendly, even illumination, natural consistent skin tones.
Color palette: faithful solid local colors from each reference; use one base color, one clean highlight, and one clean shadow per material.

Constraints: exactly five characters, one of each; official-reference identities only; same rendering style for all five; normal human ears on every character; continuous garment shapes with readable seams; clean hands and anatomy; no text, logo, or watermark.
Avoid: chibi, super-deformed proportions, fan-art redesign, pointed ears, elf ears, fantasy ears, animal ears, long ears, holographic fabric, pearlescent rainbow sheen, iridescent or prismatic color, neon skin, color contamination between characters, fragmented garments, floating fragments, shards, mosaic, collage, glitch, double exposure, broken line art, fused bodies, duplicated limbs, duplicated accessories, mixed outfits, extra people, cropped heads, text.
```
